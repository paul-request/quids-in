# Requirements — Knockout cups (Phase 12)

## Source

This feature implements Phase 12 of the quids-in roadmap:

> Add two knockout cups — our own **Quids In Cup** starting in Gameweek 16
> and the official **FPL League Cup** starting in Gameweek 35 — with a Cups
> page showing every Round 1 tie and each participant's route to the final.

Phase 12 is prioritised ahead of Phase 11 (historical seasons) because the
Quids In Cup draw must be revealed as soon as Gameweek 15 ends.

## Scope

In scope:

- A one-off, committed random draw for the Quids In Cup (GW16–19).
- Importing the official FPL League Cup (GW35–38) fixtures and results from
  FPL's public API once FPL has created them; we do not draw this cup.
- Importer additions: Gameweek finished status, FPL-style tie-break
  statistics for Quids In Cup Gameweeks, and FPL League Cup matches.
- Pure utility logic to build each cup's bracket, decide ties, advance
  winners, and gate what is revealed.
- A Cups page at `/#/cups` showing, for each cup, Round 1 player-vs-player
  ties and every later round up to the final as `Winner of Fixture 1 vs
Winner of Fixture 2`, filled in as results are decided.
- Showing each cup's Round 1 pairings as a blurred teaser until the Gameweek
  before it starts has ended, then revealing them.
- Links from the dashboard to the Cups page, and from the Cups page back to
  the dashboard.
- Cup entry money and cup prize pots in the existing Profit & loss figures.
- Documentation updates for the season-data schema and weekly workflow.

Out of scope:

- Drawing, re-drawing, or overriding the official FPL League Cup.
- Real secrecy for the Quids In Cup draw. Hiding it is only cosmetic because
  the repo and site bundle are public (see Decisions).
- Two-legged ties, replays, seeding, or manual result overrides.
- Showing cup history on the Player stats page or dashboard, beyond the
  Profit & loss figures.
- Any backend, runtime FPL request, authentication, or payment processing.

## Decisions

### Cups and schedule

| Cup            | Owner               | Round 1 | QF   | SF   | Final | Revealed after |
| -------------- | ------------------- | ------- | ---- | ---- | ----- | -------------- |
| Quids In Cup   | quids-in (our draw) | GW16    | GW17 | GW18 | GW19  | GW15 ends      |
| FPL League Cup | FPL (official)      | GW35    | GW36 | GW37 | GW38  | GW34 ends      |

- Each round lasts exactly one Gameweek.
- Both cups use a 16-slot bracket (8 → 4 → 2 → 1 ties) for the 14
  participants. Round 1 has two byes.
- The FPL League Cup's rounds, byes, and qualification are whatever FPL
  provides. The table above is the expected schedule and must be confirmed
  against the FPL data when it becomes available.

### Quids In Cup draw

- Generated **once, now**, by a local script using a cryptographically
  secure random source, and committed to the repo in a separate cup-draw
  file. The importer must never overwrite it.
- The draw assigns all 14 current participants and 2 byes to 16 slots,
  forming Fixtures 1–8. No fixture may contain two byes. The two bye slots are
  placed at random.
- A participant drawn against a bye advances to the Quarter-final
  automatically. The bye match is shown as a walkover, not a scored tie.
- The bracket is fixed at draw time. Quarter-final 1 is Winner of Fixture 1 vs
  Winner of Fixture 2, and so on. Semi-final 1 is Winner of QF1 vs Winner of
  QF2. The final is Winner of SF1 vs Winner of SF2. There is no re-draw
  between rounds.
- Re-running the draw script after the draw is committed must fail unless
  explicitly forced, so the draw cannot be regenerated by accident.

### Quids In Cup scoring and tie-breaks

- A tie is won by the participant with the higher **net score** that
  Gameweek. This is the stored scoreboard `points` value, which is already FPL
  points minus transfer cost. Transfer cost must not be subtracted again.
- If the net scores are equal, FPL's cup rules decide the tie, in this order:
  1. **Most goals scored.** Sum `goals_scored` for the 11 players who counted
     towards the team's score after automatic substitutions. Captaincy and
     other multipliers do **not** apply.
  2. **Fewest goals conceded.** Sum `goals_conceded` for the same 11 players.
  3. **Virtual coin toss.** Use a deterministic, seeded hash of the season,
     cup, round, and the two participant IDs, so every build produces the same
     result.
- A participant with no recorded score in the round's Gameweek loses. If
  neither participant has a score, the coin toss decides.
- A round's winners are only confirmed once that Gameweek has **ended**.
  Until then, scores are shown as provisional or live.

### FPL League Cup

- Fixtures, byes, scores, and winners come from FPL. Our app does not
  recalculate the winner or apply its own tie-break. FPL's `winner` field is
  authoritative.
- If FPL fixtures for a later round are not published yet, show that round as
  `Winner of Fixture N vs Winner of Fixture M`. Derive the pairing from FPL's
  Round 1 order, and replace it with FPL's own fixtures once they are
  imported. If FPL's later-round pairings differ from the derived pairing,
  FPL's fixtures win. This assumption must be verified with real GW35 data.

### "Gameweek has ended"

- A Gameweek has ended when FPL's `bootstrap-static` event for it has both
  `finished` and `data_checked` set to `true`.
- The importer records this per Gameweek in the season snapshot. The app
  does not work it out from dates or deadlines.

### Obfuscating pairings

- Before a cup's reveal Gameweek has ended, the Cups page shows a
  **teaser** of the draw:
  - the cup's name and a short meta line combining the start Gameweek with
    the reveal state, for example
    "Begins Gameweek 16 (Revealed after gameweek 15)", with the
    parenthesised status in a smaller font size;
  - the full bracket shape, with 8 Round 1 fixtures and the Winner-of
    placeholders for later rounds;
  - the real Round 1 player names, team names, and byes, rendered in place
    but **obfuscated with a CSS filter**, for example
    `filter: blur(0.35rem)`. People can see that the names are there, but
    not read them. This is meant to build excitement before the reveal.
- Obfuscated slots must:
  - be plain text, not Player/team links, so they cannot be focused,
    clicked, or hovered to reveal a destination URL;
  - be hidden from assistive technology (`aria-hidden="true"`), with a
    visually hidden text alternative such as "Hidden until Gameweek 15
    ends", so screen readers don't announce the real names;
  - use `user-select: none`, so the names can't be copied and pasted;
  - keep the blur in every colour scheme and at every breakpoint, with no
    hover, focus, or transition that removes it early;
  - show no scores, because none exist before Round 1 is played.
- Later-round slots show "Winner of Fixture N" and are not blurred. No
  real future opponent exists to hide.
- If a cup has no draw data yet (for example, FPL has not created its cup),
  there is nothing to blur. The section shows the bracket shape and a "Draw
  not yet made" message instead.
- Once revealed, the blur is removed and Round 1 shows player-vs-player ties
  as normal Player/team links. Later rounds show a participant only once the
  fixture feeding that slot has been decided. This means nobody sees a future
  opponent before the previous Gameweek ends.
- **This is cosmetic only.** The Quids In Cup draw is committed to a public
  repo and bundled into the public site. Because the obfuscated names are
  also in the page's HTML, anyone using browser developer tools, the repo,
  or the built JavaScript can read them early. This was accepted
  deliberately in favour of the teaser effect.

### Money

- Every participant pays **£1 per cup**. The season contribution changes from
  £38 to **£40** (38 Gameweeks plus 2 cups).
- Each cup's pot is £1 × the season participant count (currently £14). The cup
  winner is credited with the whole pot when the final is decided. No pot is
  split, because every tie has exactly one winner.
- Current (weekly) balance deducts each cup's £1 from the cup's first-round
  Gameweek onwards. The pot is credited only once the final has been decided.
- The Profit & loss card and the Player stats season and current
  profit/loss values keep using the one shared season-balance calculation.

### Presentation

- Route: `/#/cups`, using the existing hash routing. There is no routing
  dependency. Direct loads and refreshes must work on GitHub Pages.
- Navigation between the dashboard and the Cups page:
  - The dashboard header shows a clearly labelled, keyboard-accessible
    "🏆 Cups" button-style link to the Cups page (`#/cups`), beside the
    logo, so it can be found on mobile without scrolling. The logo stays
    centred on wider screens; on narrow screens it moves to the left of the
    header to leave room for the button. The button only appears when the
    season has cups.
  - Every sub page (Cups and Player stats) shows the same header, with a
    "← Dashboard" button-style link to `#/` in the button slot. The empty
    states keep their inline "Back to dashboard" link as an in-context
    recovery action.
  - The existing header logo link to `#/` stays unchanged.
- The page shows both cups in schedule order. Each round is labelled Round 1,
  Quarter-final, Semi-final, and Final, with its Gameweek number.
- Participant names use the existing Player/team link component.
- Winners are shown with text and an icon, not colour alone. A bye shows as
  "Bye", and an undecided slot shows as "Winner of Fixture N".
- On mobile, rounds stack vertically in order. Wider screens may show a
  horizontal bracket, with no page-level horizontal overflow at any
  supported width.

### Rules ownership

- Bracket building, tie resolution, reveal gating, and cup money are pure,
  tested utility functions. Svelte components only render the typed results.
- Derived cup results (winners, advancement) are not stored in JSON. Only
  raw inputs are stored: the draw, Gameweek finished flags, tie-break
  statistics, and FPL League Cup matches.

## Context

quids-in is a static, zero-cost scoreboard for one private FPL league. The
daily GitHub Actions workflow imports a fresh FPL snapshot and deploys it
without committing it. As a result, cup rounds update automatically within a
day of FPL finalising a Gameweek, while the Quids In Cup draw stays a
committed, reviewed file.
