# Validation — Knockout cups (Phase 12)

Each delivery slice is ready to merge when every applicable check below
passes.

## Quids In Cup draw

- [ ] `data/cup-draw-2026-27.json` is committed and was generated by the draw
      script using a cryptographically secure random source.
- [ ] It contains exactly 8 Round 1 fixtures, all 14 participants exactly
      once, exactly 2 byes, and no fixture with two byes.
- [ ] Re-running the draw script without `--force` refuses to overwrite the
      committed draw.
- [ ] The importer never modifies the draw file.

## Importer

- [ ] Every imported Gameweek has `ended` set from FPL's
      `finished && data_checked`.
- [ ] For GW16–19, each participant's score has `goalsScored` and
      `goalsConceded` summed over their 11 counting players (15 with Bench
      Boost) after automatic subs, with no captain multiplier.
- [ ] Picks and live data are only fetched for cup Gameweeks.
- [ ] `fplCup` is omitted while FPL's `cup_league` is `null`, and a failed
      FPL cup request logs a warning rather than failing the import. Once created,
      it contains every FPL cup match, including byes, de-duplicated.
- [ ] New fields are validated, and existing retry, timeout, and atomic-write
      behaviour is unchanged.
- [ ] Snapshots without the new fields still load and render.

## Cup rules

- [ ] Ties are won on net `points`, and transfer cost is not deducted twice.
- [ ] Equal net points are decided by most goals scored, then fewest goals
      conceded, then a deterministic coin toss that gives the same result on
      every build.
- [ ] Byes are walkovers. A missing score loses, and if both are missing
      the coin toss decides.
- [ ] Winners are only confirmed once the round's Gameweek has `ended`.
      Before that, scores show as provisional.
- [ ] Quarter-final, semi-final, and final pairings follow the fixed
      Winner-of links from the draw.
- [ ] FPL League Cup results use FPL's `winner` without recalculation. The
      derived Winner-of placeholders are replaced by FPL fixtures once they
      are imported.
- [ ] Unit tests cover byes, every tie-break level, missing scores,
      provisional rounds, full advancement to a champion, and no cup data.

## Obfuscated pairings

- [ ] Before GW15 has ended, the Quids In Cup section shows the bracket
      shape, a "revealed when Gameweek 15 ends" message, and the real Round 1
      names, teams, and byes blurred with a CSS filter. They are unreadable
      at phone, tablet, and desktop widths and in every colour scheme.
- [ ] Obfuscated slots are plain text, not links. They are not focusable,
      have `user-select: none`, and have no hover, focus, or transition that
      removes the blur.
- [ ] Obfuscated slots are `aria-hidden`, with a visually hidden "Hidden
      until Gameweek N ends" alternative, so screen readers never announce
      the real names.
- [ ] No scores are shown while a cup is obfuscated.
- [ ] Before GW34 has ended, the FPL League Cup section is obfuscated in
      the same way. If FPL has not created its cup yet, the section shows a
      "Draw not yet made" message.
- [ ] After the reveal Gameweek ends, the blur is removed, with no layout
      shift, and Round 1 shows player-vs-player Player/team links.
      Later-round slots only show a participant once the feeding tie is
      decided.
- [ ] The docs clearly state that obfuscation is cosmetic, because the
      names are in the page HTML, the public repo, and the bundle.

## Money

- [ ] The season contribution is £40, derived from 38 Gameweeks plus 2 cups.
- [ ] The current balance deducts £1 per cup from its first Gameweek.
- [ ] The cup winner receives the full pot (£1 × participant count) once the
      final is decided. No pot is credited before then.
- [ ] Every penny in, including Gameweek pots and cup pots, is allocated
      exactly, as proven by tests.
- [ ] Profit & loss and Player stats profit/loss use the one shared
      calculation.
